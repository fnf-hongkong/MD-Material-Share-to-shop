# Weekly Sales and Inventory

MLB HKMC weekly shop dashboard — last week's sales, SOH and weeks cover by category and SKU.
Two tabs: Normal Shops and Outlet Shops (add `#outlet` to the link to open the Outlet tab).

Live page: https://fnf-hongkong.github.io/MD-Material-Share-to-shop/Weekly%20Sales%20and%20Inventory/

Updated automatically every Monday 10:00 (HKT). Sales from Snowflake; SOH as of the Sunday of the sales week.
